## 🚀 About Me

I'm an **IaC (Infrastructure as Code) Developer** and **Sysadmin** with over **6 years of experience** in corporate environments involving **ERP**, **Linux and Windows servers**, **structured cabling**, and **requirements analysis**.

I work on **creating, reviewing, maintaining, and improving infrastructures** on **AWS** and **Azure** using modern IaC tools. I have expertise in **monitoring clusters, containers, and instances**, **CI/CD pipelines**, and observability environments with **Grafana** and **Prometheus**, always applying **security best practices**.

I'm continuously learning about **Cloud** and new technologies. Outside of work, I dedicate time to my family, music studies, and gaming. I'm a calm, curious person passionate about technology.
